# Layout and survey

A site plan freezes the shaft, access road, workstation and authorized mutation area before construction. `SiteInfrastructurePlan` describes the dry-site layout; `PerimeterInfrastructurePlan` describes a four-sided sand perimeter.

## Plan before construction

Select shaft candidates, then search for a nearby workstation and connecting route. Freeze the plan and begin world changes only after all three satisfy the construction conditions.

The shaft serves underground access. The workstation may occupy higher open space. Candidate selection considers route length, elevation, material use and actual placement feasibility.

The shaft sits beside the work area so the scaffold can connect to each working layer. The workstation is selected nearby. The dry-site plan records the shaft, bottom working stance, road and workstation volume. The perimeter plan selects a shaft cell in the west wall and isolates it with sand columns on its inner and outer sides.

## Dry shaft candidates

Inspect the entire shaft and its foundation. At every shaft height, check a horizontal radius of four blocks, using a square neighborhood that includes corners. Ordinary rock and soil can be removed under the shared mining safety rules.

The base needs stable support and room for scaffolding. Prefer natural support. A fixed foundation that can be placed directly from a safe stance is a simple construction option. Complex fluid isolation and filling to an unknown depth require assistance.

Exits must connect to the intended working layers while preserving their footing. Record foundation height separately from working-exit height.

## Elevated workstation

For a dry site, scan the whole volume downward after choosing the footprint and clearance height. Include the floor in the volume and treat blocks and fluids as occupied. Use the empty position immediately before the first collision, avoiding terrain removal for the workstation. For a sand perimeter, select a floor height above the wall and check the floor, clearance and road to the shaft.

This produces an empty-space candidate. The road, initial placement face and material budget still require validation.

## Buildable route

The road is one block wide, with a floor and two blocks of body clearance above it. Each segment must be placeable from an established support, allowing construction to continue along the completed route.

Record the exact mutation boundaries for the shaft, auxiliary stances, road and workstation before starting. Recovery uses the selected layout and rechecks the world.

## No feasible layout

If the search budget yields no plan, report the reason and relevant coordinates. The player can provide a foundation, adjust the location or assist in another way. Revalidate the conditions before continuing.

Assistance requests and completed infrastructure are reported separately.

Dry sand columns admit open air above the terrain and removable surface vegetation. The Bot reaches the actual ground, mines downward, then pillars back with sand. The start deck uses the same column capability to reach the planned height. Internal unsupported cavities remain a bounded safety refusal.

The same surface-cover owner handles two-block plants. Both halves must be loaded, match the same plant with opposite halves, and lie within the operation's authorized area. The pair is rechecked before mining starts during the approach; both native removal effects must be confirmed before the receipt records them. Outside, mismatched or unstable foundations remain refusals. Dry-column admission and execution use the same classification without treating paired plants as independently removable blocks.
